# Code of conduct

This is a small project related to a decades-old game. The bar is simple:

- Be civil. Disagree about code, not about people.
- No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, commits or review.
- Assume the other person is trying to help, and say what you mean plainly.
- Keep other people's private information out of the repository.

If someone's behaviour is a problem, mail conduct@solrlabs.com. SolrLabs LLC maintains this
repository and will decide what to do, up to blocking an account from it. Reports stay private.
